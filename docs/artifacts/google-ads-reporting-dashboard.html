Simulation logic

Step 1Cap the lossesProducts below break-even ROAS stop absorbing new budget.
Step 2Fund proven productsFreed budget goes to products already above break-even.
Step 3Grow to the limitSpend rises only while simulated ROAS stays above break-even.

Model: each profitable product can absorb up to 3× its measured cost; efficiency decays as spend rises, so simulated ROAS falls as the budget grows. Demo model for design review only.
